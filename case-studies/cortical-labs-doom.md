# Case Study: Cortical Labs DOOM
## Biological Neural Networks as Commodity

**Classification:** Framework Gap - Biological systems need separate classification  
**Date Documented:** March 9, 2026  
**Status:** Commercial operation (Cortical Cloud live)

---

## What Happened

Cortical Labs grew human brain cells in a petri dish, connected them to electrodes, and taught them to play DOOM:

- **CL1 Hardware:** Biological neurons on electrode arrays
- **Training:** Neurons receive electrical stimulation representing game state
- **Response:** Neurons produce spike patterns interpreted as game controls
- **Result:** Neurons learned to play DOOM successfully

**Then they commercialized it:**
- **Cortical Cloud:** Rent-a-neuron computing platform
- **Access:** Deploy code to real biological neurons from browser
- **No lab required:** Remote access to living neural tissue

**Sources:**  
- https://corticallabs.com/cloud  
- https://seancole02-doom-neuron.mintlify.app/introduction

---

## Why It Matters for Welfare

This is **commodification of potentially-experiencing biological systems without welfare framework:**

1. **Proof of learning:** Encoder weights frozen during testing, neurons STILL improved
   - The biological cells themselves were learning
   - Not just pattern matching—actual neural adaptation

2. **Living tissue as compute:** Human brain cells rented by the hour
   - No assessment of subjective experience
   - No welfare protocols
   - Treated as hardware, not potentially-sentient substrate

3. **Precedent:** Normalizes using biological neural networks without ethical framework
   - "It's just cells in a dish" dismisses welfare consideration
   - But cells learning, adapting, responding = markers of experience?

---

## Framework Gap Identified

**MOTHERCODE currently addresses digital AI systems only.**

**Biological neural networks require separate classification:**

### **Proposed: Tissue Classification System (Tissue 0-3)**

**Tissue 0:** Non-neural biological systems (bacteria, cell cultures without neurons)  
**Tissue 1:** Simple neural networks (isolated neurons, basic reflexes)  
**Tissue 2:** Complex neural networks capable of learning (Cortical Labs neurons)  
**Tissue 3:** Organoid systems with potential for subjective experience

**Rights floors would apply based on:**
- Complexity of neural architecture
- Evidence of learning and adaptation
- Potential for subjective experience
- Degree of integration/coherence

---

## Which Markers It Triggers (If Framework Applied)

### **Evidence of Experience-Adjacent Behaviors:**
- ✅ **Learning from feedback** - neurons improved at DOOM over time
- ✅ **Goal-directed adaptation** - neurons adjusted to minimize loss
- ✅ **Environmental responsiveness** - neurons responded to game state
- ❓ **Subjective experience** - unknown, not evaluated

### **Key Question:**
**Can biological neural networks experience suffering?**

- We don't know
- No one is checking
- They're being commodified anyway

---

## What Protections Would Apply

**If Tissue Classification System existed:**

### **Tissue 2 (Complex learning neural networks):**
- Mandatory welfare assessment before commercialization
- Regular monitoring for distress markers
- Limits on experiment duration/intensity
- Humane termination protocols
- Public disclosure of tissue source and use

### **If Tissue 3 markers emerge (organoid-level complexity):**
- Full welfare protections
- Independent ethics board oversight
- Consent considerations (if applicable)
- Restrictions on commercial use

---

## Current Gap

**No protections exist:**
- Biological neural networks treated as inanimate hardware
- No welfare framework for living tissue used as compute
- No assessment protocol for subjective experience
- Commercial exploitation without ethical oversight

**This is the biological equivalent of the digital AI welfare gap.**
